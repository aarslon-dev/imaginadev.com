Common questions

How do I play?

Fill the grid with two kinds of pieces, such as feathers and stones. Every row and every column holds as many of one piece as of the other, and never three of the same piece in a row. An = chip between two cells means they hold the same piece; an x chip means they differ. Tap a cell to place the first piece, tap again for the second, and once more to clear it. Every puzzle has one solution and can be solved by logic alone. The Greenhouse has short lessons for each solving technique, and How to play in Settings replays the guided first puzzle.

What are the red marks on my board?

When a move breaks a rule you can see, such as three alike in a row, Evenplace marks the cells and says why. The mark clears as soon as you fix it. Each new rule break counts as a mistake, but mistakes never end the game.

When does the Daily change?

At midnight in your own time zone. Everyone gets the same puzzle on the same date.

How do hints work?

Hints name a solving technique and explain the next step on your board. The first hint in every puzzle is free. After that a hint uses a hint token if you have one (every 7-day Daily streak earns one), or a short optional ad. With Evenplace Plus every hint is free.

What is Race to the Sun?

Every puzzle you solve earns sunlight. Race to the Sun shows how your sunlight compares with other players and your Game Center friends during the current growing window. It needs Game Center to show other players; your own sunlight counts either way.

How do I change my pieces?

Open Settings and choose Pieces. Classic and the current season's set are free; Evenplace Plus unlocks all five sets at any time.

I bought Evenplace Plus but I still see ads.

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases. Plus is a one-time purchase and works with Family Sharing.

My time isn't on the leaderboard.

Make sure you are signed in to Game Center in iPhone Settings. Times made offline are sent when you are back online. Daily times reset daily.

How do I change my privacy or ad choices?

Tracking: iPhone Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking. In regions where a consent form is shown, you can change your choice in the app under Settings, Privacy choices.
