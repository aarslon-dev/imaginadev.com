Evenplace Privacy Policy

Effective October 2026 · ImaginaDev LLC

In short
  • We have no servers, no accounts and no analytics of our own. Your games and progress stay on your iPhone.
  • Evenplace shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device and ad data to show and measure ads, as described below.
  • Evenplace Plus removes ads. Purchases are handled by Apple, and leaderboards by Apple's Game Center.

What stays on your iPhone

Your puzzles, times, streaks, sunlight, Greenhouse ranks and lessons, technique progress, piece set choice and settings are stored only on your iPhone. ImaginaDev LLC cannot see or access them. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Evenplace also keeps a small summary of your progress (your streak, best times, finished Dailies, Greenhouse rank, finished lessons and whether you have Evenplace Plus) in a storage area on your iPhone that other ImaginaDev games on the same iPhone can read, so they can recognize your progress. This summary also never leaves your iPhone.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Unless you have Evenplace Plus, Evenplace shows ads provided by Google AdMob: an optional short ad in exchange for a hint, and occasional full-screen ads between Endless puzzles. The Greenhouse and the lessons never show ads. To show, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect information such as your device's IP address, device type and settings, ad interactions, crash and performance data, and, only if you allow tracking, your device's advertising identifier (IDFA).

We only request ads suitable for a general audience, and we block sensitive ad categories.

Your choices:

  • Tracking. Evenplace asks for permission to track through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, the advertising identifier is not shared and you see less personalized ads. You can change this anytime in iPhone Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking.
  • Consent. In regions where it is required (such as the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland and some US states), Evenplace shows Google's consent form before any personalized ads. You can review or change your choice anytime from Settings, Privacy choices, in the app.
  • No ads at all. Evenplace Plus, a one-time purchase, removes all ads.

Google's use of this information is covered by Google's privacy policy. Learn more about how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Game Center

If you sign in to Apple's Game Center, your Game Center name, scores and achievements are sent to Apple to run the Daily time leaderboard, the Race to the Sun leaderboard and achievements. This is covered by Apple's privacy policy. You can play without signing in.

Purchases

Evenplace Plus is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We don't receive your payment details or Apple Account information.

Email

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to answer you.

Children's privacy

Evenplace is a general audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above.
