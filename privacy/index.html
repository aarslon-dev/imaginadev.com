ImaginaDev Privacy Policy

Effective November 2026 · ImaginaDev LLC

In short
  • This page covers imaginadev.com, ImaginaDev's social media accounts and the tools we use to post to them.
  • We don't sell data, run ads on this website or track you across other sites.
  • Each of our apps has its own privacy policy, linked below.

This website

imaginadev.com has no accounts, no sign-up forms, no analytics and no advertising cookies. Like any website, it is delivered by hosting services (GitHub Pages and Cloudflare), which receive your IP address and basic request details as part of delivering the pages and protecting the site from abuse.

Our social media accounts

ImaginaDev and its brands (including Sleeplings and Vistello) publish posts on platforms such as Facebook, Instagram, Threads, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn and Reddit. We publish some of these posts with our own small tool, which uses each platform's official interface and acts only on accounts that belong to ImaginaDev LLC.

Our tool does not collect, store or analyze information about people who see, like, comment on or share our posts. If you comment on or message one of our accounts, we read it only to reply and to keep our pages friendly, and the platform's own privacy policy covers what the platform collects.

Email

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to answer you.

Our apps

Each app has its own policy: Moonplace, Nineplace, Isleplace, Quiltplace, Evenplace, Heightlings and Sleeplings.

Deleting your data

We don't keep personal data from our social media tools. If you have emailed us or interacted with one of our accounts and want anything we hold about you deleted, email support@imaginadev.com with the subject "Delete my data" and we will remove it within 30 days and confirm by email.

Children's privacy

This website and our social media accounts are for a general audience and are not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above.

Contact

Questions? Email support@imaginadev.com.
