Is Moonplace for you?

What kind of puzzle is it?

Moonplace is a Star Battle style logic puzzle, the same one-per-row, one-per-column, one-per-region rules with no two pieces touching that some people know from Queens puzzles. Here you place moons on soft colored regions.

Who is it for?

Adults and teens who enjoy calm brain games, Sudoku or nonograms and want a short, relaxing daily habit. Beginners can start with the tutorial and the gentler early-week puzzles; experienced solvers can go up to 14x14 boards.

Do I need to guess?

Never. Every puzzle has exactly one solution and can be solved by logic alone.

What does it cost?

Moonplace is free. You can choose to watch a short ad for an extra hint, and Endless shows an occasional ad between puzzles. No ad ever interrupts a puzzle. Moonplace Plus is a one-time purchase ($3.99 in the US) that removes all ads for good and works with Family Sharing.

Help and support

Need help?

Email support@imaginadev.com. Tell us your iPhone model and iOS version if something isn't working.

How do I play?

Place moons so that every row, every column and every colored region has exactly one moon, and no two moons touch, not even diagonally. Tap a tile to mark an X, tap it twice to place a moon. You can replay the tutorial from Settings, How to play.

When does the Daily change?

At midnight in your own time zone. Everyone gets the same puzzle on the same date.

How do hints work?

Hints name a solving technique and explain the next step on your board. The first hint in every puzzle is free. After that a hint uses a hint token if you have one, or a short optional ad. With Moonplace Plus every hint is free.

I bought Moonplace Plus but I still see ads.

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases. Plus is a one-time purchase and works with Family Sharing.

My score isn't on the leaderboard.

Make sure you are signed in to Game Center in iPhone Settings. Scores made offline are sent when you are back online. Race to the Moon and Daily times reset daily.

How do I change my privacy or ad choices?

Tracking: iPhone Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking. In regions where a consent form is shown, you can change your choice in the app under Settings, Privacy choices.
