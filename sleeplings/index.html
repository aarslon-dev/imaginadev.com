Sleeplings

Sleep sounds for babies, toddlers and tired parents

Sleeplings plays soft, steady sounds that help little ones fall asleep, at home, in the car or away: white noise, brown noise, pink noise, car ride, fan, heartbeat, ocean waves and shushing. It keeps playing with the screen locked, fades out on a sleep timer, and turns the screen black so the light won't wake anyone. Every sound is free, with no ads and no account.

Is Sleeplings for you?

Who is it for?

Parents of babies and toddlers who want a white noise or sleep sound app for naps, bedtime and car rides, without ads, accounts or subscriptions. Tired grown-ups use it too.

What does it cost?

Every sound, the sleep timer, the sleep screen, the child lock and favorites are free, with no ads. Sleeplings Plus is a one-time purchase, not a subscription, that adds the mixer for blends of up to three sounds, every scene in full, and the sounds and scenes we add later, shared through Family Sharing.

Help and support

Need help?

Email support@imaginadev.com. Tell us your iPhone or iPad model and iOS version if something isn't working. In the app, Settings, Contact fills these in for you.

Does the sound keep playing when the screen locks?

Yes. Sleeplings keeps playing with the screen locked or while you use other apps, and you can pause it from the Lock Screen or Control Center. To hear another app at the same time, turn on Play alongside other audio in the app's Settings.

How does the sleep timer work?

Tap Sleep timer and choose how long the sound should play, or All night. When the time is up the sound fades out gently instead of stopping suddenly. You can change how long the fade takes in the app's Settings, Bedtime, Fade out.

What are the sleep screen and the child lock?

Enter sleep mode turns the screen black and dims it, so the room stays dark while the sound plays. Lock keeps little hands from changing anything: to unlock, press and hold the ring for 2 seconds (you can make it longer in the app's Settings, Hold to unlock). For a stronger lock, use Guided Access in iPhone Settings, Accessibility.

How loud should it be?

Softer than you think. Keep the phone or speaker a few feet away from the crib, not inside it, and keep the volume low. Some sounds, like the heartbeat, are deep and quiet on small phone speakers; a Bluetooth speaker or a nursery speaker brings them out.

What's free and what's in Sleeplings Plus?

Every sound, the sleep timer, the sleep screen, the child lock and favorites are free, with no ads. Sleeplings Plus is a one-time purchase that adds the mixer for your own blends of up to three sounds, every scene, and the sounds and scenes we add later. Free users can try each scene with a short preview.

I bought Plus but it isn't unlocked.

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with. Plus works with Family Sharing, so everyone in your family group gets it too.

How do I get new scenes and music?

New scenes and music appear on the Scenes tab from time to time, no app update needed. Tap Get to download one. Downloads use Wi-Fi unless you turn on Use cellular data in the app's Settings, Downloads, where you can also remove downloaded items to free up space.

Is Sleeplings on YouTube?

Yes, find long versions of our sounds and scenes at youtube.com/@sleeplings. In the app, Watch on YouTube opens the matching video.
