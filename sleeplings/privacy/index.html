Sleeplings Privacy Policy

Effective November 2026 · ImaginaDev LLC

In short
  • Sleeplings collects nothing about you or your family. No accounts, no ads, no tracking, no analytics.
  • Your favorites, blends and settings stay on your device.
  • Sleeplings Plus is a one-time purchase handled by Apple.

What stays on your device

Your favorite sounds and scenes, saved blends, volume levels, sleep timer choice and other settings are stored only on your iPhone or iPad. Scenes and music you download are stored there too. ImaginaDev LLC cannot see or access any of it. Deleting the app deletes this data.

No ads, no tracking

Sleeplings shows no ads and contains no advertising, analytics or tracking software. It does not use your advertising identifier and does not ask for permission to track you.

Downloads from imaginadev.com

To offer new scenes and music without an app update, and to keep its list of other ImaginaDev apps up to date, Sleeplings downloads small list files from imaginadev.com now and then, and the scene or music files you choose to download. These requests carry nothing about you or how you use the app. Like any web request, they are seen by the website's host (GitHub Pages), which receives your IP address as part of delivering the files.

YouTube

If you tap Watch on YouTube or the Sleeplings on YouTube link, the video opens in the YouTube app or in your browser. Sleeplings sends nothing to YouTube itself; once you are on YouTube, Google's privacy policy applies.

Purchases

Sleeplings Plus is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple through the App Store, and can be shared with your family through Family Sharing. We don't receive your payment details or Apple Account information.

Email

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to answer you.

Children's privacy

Sleeplings is made for parents and caregivers to play calming sounds for their children. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above.

Contact

Questions? Email support@imaginadev.com.
