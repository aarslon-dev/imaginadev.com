Common questions

How do I play?

Join every island into one connected archipelago. The number on each island tells how many bridges it needs. Bridges run straight across the water, never cross each other, and two islands can share one or two bridges. Drag from one island to a neighbor to build a bridge, drag again for a double, and once more to remove it. Every puzzle has one solution and can be solved by logic alone. The Chartroom has short lessons for each solving technique, and How to play in Settings replays the guided first puzzle.

What are the red marks on my board?

When a move breaks a rule you can see, such as an island with more bridges than its number or a group of islands closed off from the rest, Isleplace marks the islands and says why. The mark clears as soon as you fix it. Each new rule break counts as a mistake, but mistakes never end the game.

When does the Daily change?

At midnight in your own time zone. Everyone gets the same puzzle on the same date.

How do hints work?

Hints name a solving technique and explain the next step on your board. The first hint in every puzzle is free. After that a hint uses a hint token if you have one (every 7-day Daily streak earns one), or a short optional ad. With Isleplace Plus every hint is free.

What is Race to the Lighthouse?

Every puzzle you solve earns miles. Race to the Lighthouse shows how your miles compare with other players and your Game Center friends during the current sailing window. It needs Game Center to show other players; your own miles count either way.

How do I change the island style?

Tap the style on the Home screen, or open Settings and choose Style. Tropical and the current season's style are free; Isleplace Plus unlocks all five styles at any time.

I bought Isleplace Plus but I still see ads.

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases. Plus is a one-time purchase and works with Family Sharing.

My time isn't on the leaderboard.

Make sure you are signed in to Game Center in iPhone Settings. Times made offline are sent when you are back online. Daily times reset daily.

How do I change my privacy or ad choices?

Tracking: iPhone Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking. In regions where a consent form is shown, you can change your choice in the app under Settings, Privacy choices.
