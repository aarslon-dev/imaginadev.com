Common questions

How do I play?

Fill the grid so every row, every column and every box holds each digit exactly once. Some squares start filled; you fill the rest. Tap a square, then tap a digit. Pencil mode lets you note the digits a square could still hold, and Auto-notes fills in every possibility at once. Every puzzle has one solution and can be solved by logic alone, never by guessing. The Study has short lessons for each solving technique, and How to play in Settings replays the guided first puzzle.

What are the red marks on my board?

When the same digit appears twice in a row, column or box, Nineplace marks it and says why, for example "Two 7s in this row." The mark clears as soon as you fix it. Each new rule break counts as a mistake, but mistakes never end the game. Nineplace never checks your digits against the answer, so a wrong digit that breaks no visible rule stays quiet until it does.

When does the Daily change?

At midnight in your own time zone. Everyone gets the same puzzle on the same date. Monday is a gentle 6x6, and the week builds up to a Super Hard Saturday.

How do hints work?

Hints name a solving technique and light only the squares that prove the next step on your board. The first hint in every puzzle is free. After that a hint uses a hint token if you have one (every 7-day Daily streak earns one), or a short optional ad. With Nineplace Plus every hint is free.

What is Race to the Top Shelf?

Every puzzle you solve earns pages. Race to the Top Shelf shows how your pages compare with other players and your Game Center friends during the current window, as books climbing a bookcase. It needs Game Center to show other players; your own pages count either way.

How do I change the study style?

Tap the style on the Home screen, or open Settings and choose Style. Classic Study and the current season's style are free; Nineplace Plus unlocks all five styles at any time.

I bought Nineplace Plus but I still see ads.

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases. Plus is a one-time purchase and works with Family Sharing.

My time isn't on the leaderboard.

Make sure you are signed in to Game Center in iPhone Settings. Times made offline are sent when you are back online. Daily times reset daily.

How do I change my privacy or ad choices?

Tracking: iPhone Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking. In regions where a consent form is shown, you can change your choice in the app under Settings, Privacy choices.
