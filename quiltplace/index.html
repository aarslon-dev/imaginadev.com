Common questions

How do I play?

Split the grid into rectangles so that every rectangle holds exactly one number, and that number equals the rectangle's area. Drag from one corner to the opposite corner to draw a patch; tap a patch to remove it. Every puzzle has one solution and can be solved by logic alone. The Workshop has short lessons for each solving technique.

When does the Daily change?

At midnight in your own time zone. Everyone gets the same puzzle on the same date.

How do hints work?

Hints name a solving technique and explain the next step on your board. The first hint in every puzzle is free. After that a hint uses a hint token if you have one, or a short optional ad. With Quiltplace Plus every hint is free.

I bought Quiltplace Plus but I still see ads.

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases. Plus is a one-time purchase and works with Family Sharing.

My time isn't on the leaderboard.

Make sure you are signed in to Game Center in iPhone Settings. Times made offline are sent when you are back online. Daily times reset daily.

How do I change my privacy or ad choices?

Tracking: iPhone Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking. In regions where a consent form is shown, you can change your choice in the app under Settings, Privacy choices.
